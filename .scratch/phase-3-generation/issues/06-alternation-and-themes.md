# 06 - Resolve the alternances and place the thèmes

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03, 04

Resolve each of the six alternating créneaux to a matière from the settings of issue 02,
then fill it:

- **histoire / géographie** — a thème names its périodes in its intitulé (« (deuxième
  et troisième périodes) », « (1 ou 2 périodes au choix) »), sometimes in its
  description. Parse it; where only a count is given, walk P1…P5 giving each thème its
  minimum and handing the leftover périodes to the thèmes that allow more, in order.
  Within a période, the thèmes sharing it share its créneaux (issue 04).
- **arts plastiques / éducation musicale** — the matière is fixed per créneau; its items
  de programme are spread over the year's occurrences of that créneau.

**Done when**: all 17 thèmes are assigned périodes, each niveau alternates histoire and
géographie week by week, and the report counts both matières.
