# 05 - Place the séquences: maths, RETZ, littérature

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03, 04

- **Maths** — séquence `n` on semaine `n` (35 séquences, 36 semaines, the last is the
  marge §4.2 gives it), its four séances on the week's four maths créneaux in
  chronological order, per niveau. A week short of maths créneaux places fewer and the
  report says which.
- **RETZ** — grammaire séquences on the lundi créneau, conjugaison on the mardi one,
  each spread over its share (issue 04). Conjugaison starts only after the grammaire
  séquence on « Le verbe » ends (§4.4). CM2's classification is decided here (decision 4).
- **Littérature** — the œuvres in the order of §4.5, each starting at the first
  œuvre-suivie créneau of its période or after the previous œuvre, one séance de
  séquence per créneau. Hansel et Gretel is the repli §4.5 provides for and comes last.

**Done when**: all 35 × 2 maths and 21 × 2 RETZ séquences are placed, and the report
names every séance de séquence de littérature that found no créneau.
