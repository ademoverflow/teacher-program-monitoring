# 07 - Fill everything else: rituels and séances génériques

Type: task
Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 03, 04

- A **rituel** (a créneau of 20 minutes or less — accueil, calcul mental, lecture
  offerte, dictée du jour) repeats rather than progresses: it links every item de
  programme of its matière/domaine on every occurrence. The accueil has no matière and
  links nothing.
- Every other créneau spreads its matière's items (restricted to the créneau's domaine
  when it names one) over the year's occurrences of that (matière, domaine, niveau)
  group, in the order the programme prints them.
- The vendredi 12h00 « Dictée bilan / Poésie » additionally links the poésie entrée of
  Culture littéraire et artistique on every occurrence (decision 6).

**Done when**: every créneau of every taught day carries a séance, and no séance links
an item de programme of a matière other than its own.
