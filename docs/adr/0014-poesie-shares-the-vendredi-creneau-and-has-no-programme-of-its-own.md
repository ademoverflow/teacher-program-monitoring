# Poésie shares the vendredi créneau and has no programme of its own

The vendredi 12h00-12h30 créneau is printed « Dictée bilan / Poésie ». It stays one
séance, in français/orthographe as the timetable seed has it, and it links two things:
the orthographe items the year's progression has reached, and — on every occurrence —
« Savourer le goût des mots, imaginer et créer en poésie », the poésie entrée of
« Culture littéraire et artistique ».

Making it a third alternance was the obvious alternative, and it is wrong on the source:
§4.1 lists exactly two alternances, and ADR-0002 gave an `alternation_group` to exactly
six créneaux, of which this is not one. Splitting the 30 minutes in two was the other,
and the EDT prints one cell of 30 minutes, not two of 15 (ADR-0003).

The matière `poesie` therefore keeps its row in `subjects` — it is the teacher's word and
it has a colour — and keeps its zero items de programme, which is not an oversight: the
programme officiel has no poésie section, it has a poésie entrée inside français. No
séance is filed under `poesie`; the poésie taught on a Friday is filed under français and
is traceable to the item de programme that covers it.
