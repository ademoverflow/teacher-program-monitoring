# The curriculum is read by script where the PDF has text, and by hand where it does not

`core/seed/program_items.json` is generated by `scripts/extract_program_items.py`, which reads
`docs/programme-cm1-cm2.pdf` through `pdftotext -layout` and copies what it finds. Pages 90-107
(sciences et technologie) carry no text layer at all — they are images — so they were read in
vision and transcribed into `scripts/curriculum_sciences.json`, which the script merges in. The
script and its mapping tables stay in the repo, and the seed is regenerated by re-running it.

The alternative was to hand-write the seed once and delete the tooling. We kept the script
because §10 forbids inventing pedagogical content, and a script that copies substrings cannot
paraphrase: the guardrail is mechanical rather than a promise. It also makes the seed checkable —
a reviewer can re-run the extraction and diff — and it makes the classification decisions
explicit, since every mapping from a heading of the source to one of our domaines is a line in
`scripts/curriculum_map.py` rather than a judgement buried in the data.

The price is that the seed is only as good as what `pdftotext` recovers, and what it does not
recover is recorded rather than left to be rediscovered. Eight items are marked `needs_review`
because their table did not split into its columns: the text is the PDF's, but two columns share a
line instead of following one another. Two things are not extracted at all. The histoire des arts
programme (p. 151-154) is out: it is a cycle-3 transversal teaching with no créneau in the
timetable and no matière in §5, so there is nowhere to put it. And « Initiation à la pensée
informatique » gets a domaine's worth of pages but no item, because the source gives it prose and
no objectives, saying its content is « abordé dans les autres domaines de ce programme ».
