# The œuvres are laid end to end, and the year is three créneaux short

The eight œuvres of §4.5 are read on the « Lecture — Œuvre suivie » créneau of the lundi,
one séance de séquence per créneau, in the order §4.5 prints them. An œuvre starts at the
first œuvre-suivie créneau of the période §4.5 gives it, or later if the previous œuvre
is still running; it never starts earlier.

The year does not have room for the planning. The eight œuvres carry 36 séances de
séquence between them and the year has 33 lundis travaillés — S1 has no lundi at all, and
two more are chômés. Worse, the périodes and the plannings do not line up even where the
lundis exist: P3 is five semaines long and is given ten semaines of œuvres.

The generator therefore reports what it cannot place instead of working around it (§10).
What falls off the end is « Jumanji », semaine 3 and the two semaines of « Hansel et
Gretel ». That is not an accident of the ordering: §4.5 itself calls Hansel « (repli en P5
si manque de temps) », so it is read last, after Jumanji and Zathura, and it is the first
thing the year drops when time runs out — which is what the source says should happen.
« Zathura » is reported separately: the source gives it no week-by-week planning at all,
so there is nothing to place.

The alternative was to anchor each œuvre strictly inside its période and drop whatever
overflowed. It places eight fewer séances and leaves five lundis of P5 with nothing to
read, and it loses Charlie's opening week and two of Le Magicien d'Oz's rather than the
tail the source marks as optional. Laying them end to end covers all 33 créneaux and
loses only the last three steps.
