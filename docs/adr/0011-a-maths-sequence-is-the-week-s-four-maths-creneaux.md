# A maths séquence is the content of all four maths créneaux of its semaine

Séquence *n* of `maths-cm1` and of `maths-cm2` is taught in semaine *n*, and its four
séances take the week's four « Mathématiques » créneaux in the order they run — lundi,
mardi, jeudi, vendredi. §4.3 states it outright: a séquence is « une suite de 4 séances
(lun/mar/jeu/ven, sur les créneaux « Mathématiques ») ». The méthodo never titles those
four séances, so a séance is named after its séquence and its rank: « Séquence 7 —
Droites perpendiculaires (séance 2/4) ».

The alternative was to read the EDT's own labels as the plan — lundi Nombres, mardi
Calculs, jeudi Grandeurs et mesures, vendredi Géométrie — and hang the week's séquence
only on the créneau whose domaine matches it. It is tempting because it explains the
labels, and we rejected it because it leaves three créneaux out of four with nothing the
sources provide: filling them would have meant inventing maths lessons, which §10
forbids, and leaving them empty would have meant 105 empty créneaux a year per niveau.

So the domaine printed on a maths créneau stays what it always was — where the cell sits
in the grid — and is not a claim about what the week's séquence covers. A séance of
« Droites perpendiculaires » really does fall in the cell labelled « Mathématiques —
Nombres » in the weeks where the séquence is a geometry one. The tension is the EDT's and
the méthodo's, not ours, and the EDT is immutable (§10).

## And the ateliers problèmes créneau carries no séquence

§8 Phase 3 sums the maths rule up as « séquences … réparties sur les 4 créneaux maths **+
ateliers problèmes** », which reads as five créneaux. §4.3, which is the méthodo itself,
lists them apart: « 4 séances de séquence (lun/mar/jeu/ven, sur les créneaux
« Mathématiques »), calcul mental quotidien, ateliers problèmes (créneau du mardi 15h00
dans l'EDT), Flash Maths 5' ». The ateliers are one element of the weekly structure
alongside the séquence, not a fifth séance of it, and the méthodo gives them no content
of their own. So the mardi 15h00 créneau is filled from the programme instead — « La
résolution de problèmes », at both niveaux (ADR-0015) — and it is commun, because nothing
per niveau drives it (ADR-0010).

Stretching a four-séance séquence over five créneaux would have invented a fifth séance,
and hanging the week's séquence on the atelier as "related work" would have been our claim
rather than the méthodo's. Where the summary and the source disagree, §10 says the source
wins.

## When the semaine is short

A semaine with fewer than four maths créneaux places fewer séances and the report says
which: S1 has no lundi, and S25, S28 and S30 lose a day to a jour chômé. Five séances de
séquence a niveau are lost that way, and none of them is quietly moved somewhere else.
